FAQ

What people usually ask before starting.

I don’t understand tech at all. Will I be able to use it? +

Yes — for two reasons. First, you don’t have to set anything up alone: on Co-Pilot and Auto-Pilot our team puts everything live for you in 7 days (on Solo Pilot, the AI guides you step by step, and Turbo Setup builds it for you for R$ 497). Second, MyDose School teaches you the rest. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use MyDose.

Will my patients actually use the app? +

That’s our job number 1. The app works like a game: the patient logs the habits in your plan, earns points, joins challenges and sees the community alongside them. Average result: 3× more adherence — and a patient who sticks comes back, renews and refers.

What are credits and what can I create with them? +

Credits are the “energy” the AI uses to create your content: posts, videos, e-books, scripts and even follow-up care plans. Every plan already comes with monthly credits. Need more in a busier month? Buy a pack from R$ 49, without touching your plan — same as buying more WhatsApp or more recording hours.

I don’t know how to sell or price. Does MyDose solve that? +

Yes — that’s exactly why MyDose School exists, included in every plan. It teaches what school never did: pricing your programs, building follow-up care plans, creating digital products and using AI (Claude and ChatGPT) to make e-books and scripts. Health entrepreneurship, step by step. And on Co-Pilot and Auto-Pilot, a specialist does it alongside you.

How soon do I see results? +

Within 7 days everything is live. In 30 days you make your 1st sale inside MyDose — or we work for free until it happens. And on Auto-Pilot, if in 90 days the plan doesn’t pay for itself, we refund the difference.

Any lock-in or penalty? +

No lock-in traps. You get a full 30-day guarantee: if you don’t love it, we refund 100%, no questions. And you can move up or down a plan whenever you want.

Is the ad budget included in the price? +

No, and that’s on purpose: the budget stays transparent and under your control, outside the subscription — you always know how much goes to ads and how much is our work. On Co-Pilot and Auto-Pilot, our team runs the ads for you with that budget.

Which plan is for me? +

Simple: want to go at your own pace paying little, with AI and the School on your side? Solo Pilot. Want the team by your side creating content and running ads? Co-Pilot (the most chosen). Want to hand it all over and just keep your patients? Auto-Pilot. Not sure — start smaller; you can move up whenever, no penalty.

The AI is trained within your limits, with ethics and confidentiality. It keeps the bond between sessions without replacing your clinical work — you set the tone and the rules.
