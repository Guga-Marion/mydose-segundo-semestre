Do I need to understand tech or marketing? +

No. With Software everything is ready and simple; on service plans (Co-pilot, Autopilot, Partner) we handle content, ads and strategy for you. You just care for people.

I have few patients today. Is it still worth it? +

Yes — that's exactly when it pays off most. The community boosts adherence and repeat sales from patients you already have, and the product ladder creates revenue without needing more slots.

What about ethics and confidentiality, especially in mental health? +

The AI is trained within your limits, with ethics and confidentiality. It keeps the bond between sessions without replacing your clinical work — you set the tone and the rules.

Any lock-in or cancellation fee? +

No lock-in traps. And you get a 30-day guarantee: if you don't love it, we refund 100%, no questions asked.

How soon do I see results? +

Our promise: within 30 days you make your first sale inside MyDose and have patients using your protocols — or we work for free until it happens.

No — on purpose: ad spend stays transparent and under your control, outside the subscription. You always know how much goes to ads.
