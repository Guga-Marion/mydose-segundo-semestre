Hello User, thank you for using the MyDose app. My Dose LLC is committed to complying with the laws that protect the privacy of Users' data and the general public. To provide the services related to the MyDose app, it is necessary to process and share personal data. BY USING THE MYDOSE APP, THE USER TACITLY AND FULLY AGREES TO THE PROVISIONS CONTAINED HEREIN.

Chapter IGeneral Provisions

1.1. The MyDose app places the utmost importance on the relationship with its Users and their personal data. This Privacy Policy was created to communicate our practices for collecting, using and disclosing information.

1.2. Personal information is data provided by Users that can be used to identify them individually.

1.3. Sensitive personal information includes data on racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, political opinions and health data, as set out in Law 13.709/18 (LGPD).

1.4. BY USING THE MYDOSE APP, YOU AUTHORIZE THE COLLECTION, USE AND DISCLOSURE OF INFORMATION ABOUT YOU, UNDER THE TERMS OF THIS POLICY.

1.5. The MyDose app operates in compliance with Brazilian legislation, including Law No. 12.965/2014 (Brazilian Civil Rights Framework for the Internet) and Law No. 13.709/2018 (LGPD).

1.6. If you have any questions, please contact us at contact@mydoseapp.com.

1.7. This Policy describes what information may be collected about Users and how it may be used.

Chapter IIInformation Collected

2.1. Data collected from Users:

  • Full name
  • Valid e-mail address
  • Date of birth
  • Age
  • Physical health, mental wellbeing, personal budget and nutrition information

2.1.1. Personal data is collected in various circumstances, including registration in the app and activation of features.

2.1.2. Sensitive personal data is also collected, such as physical activity data, medical and health information from connected devices (Google Fit, Apple Health, Samsung Health, Strava, Garmin, etc.).

2.1.3. Users may choose to connect and share information from partner apps with MyDose.

2.1.4. Information collected by partners and third parties is subject to their own terms and policies.

2.2. Data collected from Leader Users:

  • Full name
  • Valid e-mail address
  • Phone
  • Tax ID (CPF or CNPJ)
  • Full address
  • Credit card data

2.3. Collection of minors' data will be carried out with the consent of the Legal Guardian.

2.4. Data obtained through Google APIs. When a Leader User chooses to connect their Google account, MyDose requests access to the following Google API scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events — to create calendar events (with a Google Meet link) in the professional's Google Calendar when an online appointment is scheduled on the platform.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email — to identify and display which Google account was connected.

The processing of all data obtained through these Google APIs is governed exclusively by Chapter XIV (Google User Data — Limited Use) below, which prevails over any provision to the contrary in this Policy.

Chapter IIIHow We Use Information

3.1. MyDose uses the collected information to:

  • a) Identify and register Users and provide the app's services
  • b) Ensure content is presented in the most efficient way
  • c) Make general improvements to the app
  • d) Give HR Administrators access to data for analysis and reports
  • e) Contact and notify Users about changes to the platform
  • f) Conduct statistical research
  • g) Feed the MyDose Data Lake database
  • h) Promotional or marketing purposes
  • i) Develop personalized solutions for a healthier life

3.2. Additional non-personal information may be used for any purpose.

3.3. MYDOSE KEEPS INTERNET ACCESS RECORDS CONFIDENTIAL, IN A CONTROLLED AND SECURE ENVIRONMENT, WITH ENCRYPTION IN TRANSIT.

3.4. Sensitive medical information will not be used in future underwriting or claims decisions.

3.5. MyDose services are not intended to diagnose, cure, mitigate, treat or prevent any condition, nor do they replace professional medical care.

3.6. The uses described in items 3.1(d), 3.1(f), 3.1(g), 3.1(h) and 3.2 do NOT apply to data obtained through Google APIs. That data is used solely as described in Chapter XIV.

Chapter IVSharing of Information

4.1. MyDose does not disclose to third parties any information provided by the User, except:

  • a) To comply with a legal obligation or prevent fraud
  • b) To protect MyDose's rights, property or safety
  • c) With police or governmental authorities
  • d) Upon prior notice and consent of the User
  • e) By the User's own action, such as sharing with Community Leaders
  • f) For statistical or authorial research
  • g) With third-party service providers in compliance with privacy standards
  • h) With agents, contractors or third-party service providers
  • i) With contracted third-party servers that store the database (Google Cloud Platform, Supabase)
  • j) In case of sale, merger or corporate restructuring

4.2. International sharing. MyDose uses Google Cloud Platform services to store and process data, which may be transferred to the United States.

4.3. Sharing with AI. User data (excluding data obtained through Google APIs) may be shared via API integration with Gemini (Google AI) for reports, process automation and automated interactions. Data obtained through Google APIs is never transmitted to AI services, as per Chapter XIV.

4.4. Non-disclosure. Except in the situations above, MyDose will not disclose any personal information.

4.5. THE USER HAS THE RIGHT NOT TO ACCEPT THESE TERMS AND TO REMOVE THEIR DATA.

4.6. By accepting these terms, the User authorizes the sharing of data. The User may revoke consent at any time, including by disconnecting their Google account.

4.7. MyDose will retain the User's data for as long as the User remains registered or while there is a legitimate interest of the company.

4.8. The sharing permitted by items 4.1(f), 4.1(g), 4.1(h) and 4.3 does NOT apply to data obtained through Google APIs, except when strictly necessary to provide user-facing functionality, as detailed in Chapter XIV.

Chapter XIVGoogle User Data — Limited Use

MyDose's use and transfer of any information received from Google APIs — including the calendar.events and userinfo.email scopes — adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, data obtained through Google APIs is used solely to provide and improve the user-facing features for which the user granted access. The calendar.events scope is used only to create calendar events (with a Google Meet link) in the professional's Google Calendar when an online appointment is scheduled on the platform. The userinfo.email scope is used only to identify and display which Google account was connected.

MyDose does not:

  • use Google user data for advertising, marketing or any promotional purpose;
  • sell Google user data, nor transfer it to third parties, except as necessary to provide or improve user-facing functionality, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to the user;
  • use or transfer Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence and/or machine learning models, including third-party AI services such as Gemini;
  • allow humans to read Google user data, except when: (a) the user provides prior explicit consent; (b) it is necessary for security purposes; (c) it is required to comply with applicable law; or (d) the data is aggregated and anonymized and used for internal operations.

This Chapter XIV prevails over any provision to the contrary in this Policy. The provisions of Chapters III, IV, VIII, XI and XII regarding marketing, data aggregation, Data Lake, commercial use, third-party sharing and AI processing do not apply to data obtained through Google APIs.

Users may revoke MyDose's access to their Google data at any time by disconnecting their Google account within the app, or via the Google Account permissions page.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MyDose's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. MyDose does not use or transfer data obtained through Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence and/or machine learning models.
